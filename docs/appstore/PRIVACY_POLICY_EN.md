# Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 26, 2026

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how okiniirinoao ("we", "us", or "our") handles user privacy in connection with the Pomodoro timer application "Kokukoku" (the "App").

## Data Collection

**The App does not collect any personal data whatsoever.**

Specifically, we do not collect, transmit, or store (on external servers) any of the following:

- Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or other contact information
- Location data
- Device identifiers (IDFA, IDFV, etc.)
- Usage analytics
- Advertising data
- Health or fitness data
- Financial information
- Any other personal information

## Data Storage

All data generated by the App (timer settings, session history, etc.) is stored locally on the user's device. This data is never transmitted to any external server.

## Network Communication

The App does not communicate with any external servers. We do not use any third-party analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, crash reporting services, or any other third-party data collection tools.

## Device-to-Device Sync

The App uses Apple's WatchConnectivity framework to synchronize timer state between iPhone and Apple Watch. This communication occurs directly between paired devices through Apple's system frameworks and does not pass through our servers.

## Device Permissions

The App may request access to the following device capabilities. Each requires explicit user consent before use.

### Notifications
We request permission to send local notifications to alert you when a timer session completes. Notifications are processed entirely on-device; no data is sent to external servers.

### Focus Status
The App reads the system Focus Mode status to automatically mute notification sounds while Focus is active.

### Audio Playback
The App uses AVAudioEngine to generate ambient pink noise during focus sessions. No audio recording occurs. The App does not access the device microphone.

## User Accounts

The App does not require or support account registration or login.

## Supported Platforms

The App is available on iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Watch as a Universal Purchase. This Privacy Policy applies to the App on all supported platforms.

## Purchases

The App is a one-time paid purchase. There are no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

## Children's Privacy

Because the App collects no personal data, it is safe for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction).

## GDPR (European Economic Area)

Since the App does not collect, process, or store any personal data, GDPR obligations regarding data processing do not apply. No personal data is transferred outside the user's device. There is no data controller or data processor relationship, as no data is collected.

If you are located in the EEA and have questions about your rights under GDPR, please contact us at the address below.

## CCPA (California)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act, California residents have the right to know what personal information is collected about them. The App collects no personal information as defined by the CCPA. We do not sell personal information. Because no data is collected, there is no data to disclose, delete, or opt out of.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify users through the App or on the hosting page. The revised policy takes effect upon posting.
